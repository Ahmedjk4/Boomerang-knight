One knight. One sword. Don’t lose track of it.

Boomerang Knight is a top-down survival game where your sword is also your boomerang. Charge your throw, cut through approaching enemies, and catch the blade on its return, or rush across the arena to retrieve it. Until you get it back, all you can do is dodge.

Survive increasingly difficult enemy waves, manage your stamina, and grab explosive power-ups to break through a crowd. Play solo or team up with a friend in local co-op, where clearing a phase brings your fallen teammate back into the fight.
